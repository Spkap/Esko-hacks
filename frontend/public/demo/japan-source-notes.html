Scope and interpretation

Category, fruit presentation, composition basis, wording, prominence, and exceptions all matter. A low-juice declaration is not restricted to percentage text. The threshold depends on the specified measurement basis; an unknown basis stays uncertain. Physical text size and assembled-package visibility cannot be established from this digital layout.
